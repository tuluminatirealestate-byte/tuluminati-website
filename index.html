Financing FAQ

Flexible options for foreigners and Mexicans — we help you find the best path to ownership

Best Financial Option
🏗️

Developer Financing

1-2 Years Up to 5 Years (select developers)

The smartest choice for most buyers. Pay directly to the developer during construction with 0% interest in many cases. Typically requires 30-50% down payment with the balance spread over construction period.

  • No bank approval needed — available to all nationalities
  • No credit check — ideal for foreigners without Mexican credit history
  • 0% interest — many developers offer interest-free payments
  • Flexible terms — monthly, quarterly, or milestone-based payments
  • Pre-sale prices — lock in today's price, pay over time
Example: $300,000 property = $90,000 down + $8,750/month for 24 months
🏦

Mexican Bank Mortgages

10-20 Years For Foreigners & Mexicans

Traditional bank financing through Mexican institutions. Longer terms mean lower monthly payments, but higher total interest paid. Available to both foreign nationals and Mexican citizens.

  • Long terms — 10, 15, or 20 year mortgages available
  • Lower monthly payments — spread cost over many years
  • For foreigners — banks like Scotiabank, HSBC Mexico, and Santander offer foreigner programs
  • For Mexicans — more options available with better rates
  • Higher interest rates — typically 9-12% for foreigners, 8-11% for Mexicans
Note: Interest rates are higher than US/Canada but property taxes are 50x lower — the math still works!
🌎

Cross-Border Mortgages

15-30 Years US/Canada Residents

Leverage your US or Canadian credit to finance Mexican property. Partner lenders specialize in cross-border transactions for North American buyers.

  • Use your US/Canadian credit — no Mexican credit history needed
  • Competitive rates — starting at 7.5% APR
  • Familiar process — similar to home country mortgage
  • Dollar-denominated — avoid currency exchange risk
🔐

Fideicomiso (Bank Trust)

Required for Foreigners 50-Year Renewable

Not financing per se, but essential for foreign ownership within 50km of coastline. A Mexican bank holds title in trust while you retain all ownership rights — use, sell, rent, inherit, or modify the property.

  • Full ownership rights — you control everything
  • Setup cost — approximately $2,000-3,000 USD one-time
  • Annual fee — approximately $500-800 USD/year
  • Renewable — 50-year terms, indefinitely renewable
